# EXP-INT-001 — GRIP Beauty Opportunity Intelligence Test

**Created:** 2026-05-30  
**Status:** Designed / not yet executed  
**Track:** Market Intelligence  
**Decision supported:** Whether GRIP Beauty deserves to become the first sellable vertical and what its initial offer should be.

## 1. Question

Is a WhatsApp booking + deposit + reminder/rebooking system for peluquerías/barberías a sufficiently strong first GRIP offer in Mendoza/Argentina, compared with other initial vertical candidates?

## 2. Why this experiment exists

The founder prefers practical execution and has an immediate intuitive interest in selling to barberías/peluquerías. This is useful for speed, but GRIP must distinguish a good demo idea from a validated first market.

## 3. Research objectives

### International intelligence

Find public examples of companies/agencies/products selling systems to salons or barbers around:

- online or messaging-based appointments;
- deposit/prepayment/no-show protection;
- reminders and cancellations;
- rebooking and retention;
- reviews/reputation;
- AI receptionist or automated inbound handling.

For each relevant example record:

- company/product;
- country/market;
- exact offer;
- target business;
- visible promise/headline;
- workflow/functionality shown;
- visible price or business model, if available;
- CTA/demo style;
- source evidence;
- insight applicable to GRIP, without copying brand assets.

### Local opportunity intelligence

Identify lawful, public signals from Mendoza-area peluquerías/barberías that suggest potential fit for GRIP Beauty.

Candidate public signals:

- publicly listed WhatsApp or messaging channel;
- booking handled only through manual messaging, where visible;
- public comments/reviews mentioning waiting, scheduling, response difficulty or no-shows, if present;
- active Instagram presence but no visible automated booking route;
- multiple branches or high review volume indicating operational scale;
- no visible deposit/reservation system.

Do not infer internal business problems without evidence. Do not collect unnecessary personal customer data.

## 4. Outputs required

| Output | Minimum standard |
|---|---|
| International offer matrix | 15 relevant, evidenced examples across at least 2 mature markets |
| Repeated offer patterns | At least 5 recurring problems/promises/functions |
| Local candidate set | 30 public business candidates with observable signals only |
| Initial ICP hypothesis | Type of barbería/peluquería most likely to benefit |
| Offer recommendation | What GRIP Beauty should demonstrate first |
| Tool benchmark data | Which provider/source found each result, time/cost/quality notes |
| Decision | Proceed, narrow, compare with another vertical first, or reject |

## 5. Provider benchmark principle

This experiment is also the first test of intelligence tooling. No provider is selected in advance. Each tool/source evaluated must be judged by:

- relevance of results;
- evidence quality and traceability;
- structured output usefulness;
- ability to repeat or monitor;
- cost per useful finding;
- compliance/privacy risk;
- ease of integration into GRIP's operating system.

## 6. Tools/sources to evaluate

Candidate classes, not final decisions:

- public web research with cited evidence;
- structured search/local/ads/keyword data providers;
- research/list-building APIs;
- self-controlled extraction for already-selected public pages;
- direct manual validation of high-value findings.

Named providers may be tested only after their capability and trial/cost constraints are verified against this exact experiment.

## 7. Decision threshold

GRIP Beauty proceeds as the first implementation candidate only when evidence suggests:

- a clear and communicable pain;
- an offer that can be demonstrated reliably;
- accessible local potential customers;
- operational complexity manageable for an initial pilot;
- a recurring value path after installation;
- no stronger obvious initial vertical found in parallel comparison.

## 8. Next execution action

Before purchasing tooling or building the workflow, prepare the first international offer matrix using public sources and define the local discovery method for Mendoza with data minimization and traceable evidence.
