**Created:** 2026-05-30  
**Corrected:** 2026-05-30

**Track:** Catalog and Market Intelligence  
**Decision supported:** Which individual automations GRIP should demo and offer first.

## 1. Correction of purpose

This experiment no longer aims to decide whether `GRIP Beauty` becomes GRIP's first product vertical.

GRIP sells **individual and combinable automations**. The task is to determine which automation modules are easiest to communicate, demonstrate, implement safely and sell across different types of business.

Rubros such as peluquerías, hotelería or gastronomía are useful contexts for examples and prospecting, not mandatory product categories.

## 2. Central question

Which three to five automation modules should GRIP build as its first demos and initial commercial catalog?

## 4. Research objectives

### A. International commercial intelligence

Find public examples of agencies/products offering automations such as:

- lead capture and CRM entry;
- appointment/reservation creation;
- reminders and no-show prevention;
- reviews and re-engagement;
- automated customer replies;
- operational reporting;
- notifications and system synchronization.

For each relevant example record:

- company/product;
- country/market;
- automation being sold;
- business context/rubro used in their explanation;
- visible promise/headline;
- functionality shown;
- visible price or commercial model, when available;
- CTA/demo style;
- source evidence;
- idea useful to GRIP without copying proprietary branding/assets.

### B. Practical applicability by rubro

For each strong automation module, identify at least three business contexts where it can be demonstrated.

Example:

| Automation | Peluquería | Hotel | Servicio profesional |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recordatorio | turno de corte | check-in/reserva | cita o visita |
| CRM | consulta por turno | interesado en estadía | nuevo presupuesto |
| Reseña | post-servicio | post-estadía | trabajo finalizado |

### C. Local selling opportunity

Identify lawful, public signals from Mendoza-area businesses that may benefit from one or more modules, without claiming problems that are not observable.

Signals may include:

- a public booking/contact channel;
- active demand/reviews;
- manual-looking enquiry path, where observable;
- absence of a visible booking form or structured next step;
- multiple locations or considerable public activity;
- public reliance on contact forms, WhatsApp or social DMs.

Do not collect unnecessary personal data and do not infer internal operational failures without evidence.

## 6. Provider benchmark principle

No provider is selected in advance. Each tool/source evaluated must be judged by:

- relevance and completeness of results;
- evidence quality and traceability;
- usefulness for organizing the automation catalog;
- ability to repeat or monitor discovery;
- cost per useful result;
- compliance/privacy risk;
- ease of integration into GRIP's operating system.

## 7. Decision threshold for first demo modules

An automation enters first-demo construction only when it has:

- a simple outcome any business owner understands;
- usefulness in more than one rubro, unless a specific niche advantage is evidenced;
- manageable implementation and safety risk;
- a clear visual demo path;
- evidence that similar outcomes are sold or needed in the market;
- a defined scope that can be sold alone.
